Heart Swap :: Releases

Heart Swap is my music band solo project, usually in some punk flavor of rock music. I am inspired by acts like Cheekface, Black Dresses, Beat Happening, Crush Fund, Car Seat Headrest, Guided by Voices, and Pavement. I currently handle everything myself, including lead guitar, rhythm guitar, bass, synths, stanky GBA soundfont samples, vocals, lyrics, production, and web design.

I'm almost done with my debut album! It is currently slated for release in Fall 2026. You can listen to some year-old demos of songs that will be on that album, plus various other stuff I've made, on my Soundcloud below.

Heart Swap · Everything so far (Summer 2025)

In 2024, I contributed a cover of the Cheekface song "Sexy National Anthem" under my old artist name for a charity compilation album benefitting several queer/trans orgs. Then in 2025, I also contributed a cover of the Cheekface song "Still Life" for a charity release benefitting several immigrant and queer justice orgs.
